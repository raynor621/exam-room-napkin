Bring your MRI

Bring the images, not just the report: a disc or a link to the images, plus the written report. Bring X-rays if you have them, a list of the injections you have had in that shoulder with approximate dates, and how much physical therapy you have done. If the scan is more than a few months old and surgery is being considered, I will usually order a new one.

If you live outside Dallas-Fort Worth, my team and I will often offer to review your images before you travel and let you know whether a trip to Dallas for surgery is likely to be worth it. For insurance reasons this review cannot be an official telemedicine visit, but it can help you decide whether to make the trip.

Things to consider

  • When was your MRI done, and has anything changed since?
  • Did it start with a fall or an injury, or come on gradually?
  • Can you lift your arm overhead? Has it felt weaker?
  • What have you already tried: therapy, medicine, a shot? How many shots?
  • What do you want to get back to doing?

Questions you may want to ask me

  • Is my tear partial or full-thickness, and how big is it?
  • Has the tendon pulled back, and how healthy is the muscle?
  • Did my tear wear in over time, or come from an injury?
  • Does my MRI change what we should try first?
  • Is my MRI recent enough, or would I need a new one?
