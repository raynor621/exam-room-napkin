Meniscus repair roadmap ▶Knee arthroscopy (a trim) ▶

Think about

  • Did it start with a twist or an injury, or come on gradually?
  • Where exactly does it hurt? Be ready to point to it.
  • Does it catch, lock or swell?
  • What have you already tried: therapy, medicine, a shot?
  • What do you want to get back to doing?

Good questions to ask me

  • Is my tear one that could be repaired, or would it be trimmed?
  • Is physical therapy or an injection a good place to start?
  • Is there anything I should avoid while it settles down?
  • If I need surgery, what would my recovery look like?
